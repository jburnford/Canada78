# Phase 0 Crosswalk Audit — 2026-08-23

Produced by `build/audit_crosswalks.py` (rerunnable). First pass over the
source corpora to establish identifier coverage and seed the registries.

## Findings

1. **Catalog temporal ceiling: sessions 1867–1900 (seq 1–34).** The parsed
   session indexes stop at 1900, so any document presented after 1900
   cannot yet get a `paper_id`. Affects 31/51 DIA volumes (report years
   1900–1930) — assigned `prov:` ids in the document registry, to be
   reconciled (not renumbered) when the catalog extends. Extending the
   catalog past 1900 rides on the OCR parallel track.

2. **DIA crosswalk: 20/20 linkable volumes now linked** (17 auto via
   `sessional_links.json`, 3 recovered by curation). The 3 misses (report
   years 1881–1883) were caused by index-title mangling — the session
   indexes for 1882–1884 read "Annual Report of the Department of, for the
   calendar year…", losing the department name. Recovered via the
   catalog's own series attribution (`1882_6`, `1883_5` are tagged
   `annual_report:indian_affairs`) and DIA's distinctive
   calendar-year-end phrasing (`1884_4`, confidence medium — verify when
   that volume's text is extracted). See
   `curation/dia_sessional_overrides.csv`.

3. **T&N crosswalk: 8/17 staged volumes resolve to paper_ids** (7 with
   extracted catalog text, 1 index-only). The 9 unresolved are all
   pre-1876 sessions (seq ≤ 8), whose indexes do not carry a "Tables of
   the Trade and Navigation" title — early sessions title the returns
   differently or the index parse missed them. Bounded follow-up: inspect
   seq 1–8 indexes for the variant title.

4. **The catalog pinpoints the missing T&N fiscal years.** 15 T&N papers
   are indexed in the catalog but absent from `uk_trade_db/raw_canada` —
   including exactly the known gap years (fiscal 1875→seq 9, 1876→10,
   1878→12, 1881→15, 1886→20, 1888→22) plus fiscal 1890–1899 (seq 24–34)
   not yet staged. Once those sessions' volumes clear OCR, the gap volumes
   can be pulled directly by (seq, paper_num).

5. **Upstream bug to report (sessional_papers enrichment):** `1885_153a`
   (Annual Report of the NWMP Commissioner) is misattributed to series
   `annual_report:indian_affairs`. The NWMP report was historically bound
   with Indian Affairs matter in some years, but it belongs to
   `annual_report:mounted_police`.

6. **Person overlap probe (weak evidence, as expected).** 162 DIA agent
   surnames (1885/1895/1905 volumes) vs 10,798 Colonial Office List
   surnames → 98 surname-level collisions, which at surname granularity is
   mostly noise. Real person matching (Phase 1) needs the LINCS Indian
   Affairs Agents dataset plus given names/dates; no evidence yet of
   meaningful COL↔DIA career overlap.
